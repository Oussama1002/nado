2. Buyer experience & discovery
Saved searches + alerts (M, reuses demandes concept). Buyers save a filter ("BMW Série 3, <200k km, <250k MAD, Casablanca") and get notified (email now, WhatsApp later) when a match is posted. Your demandes forum is halfway there — formalize it as alerts.
Favorites / wishlist (S). Needs client auth (you have it). Drives return visits and powers "price dropped on a car you saved" notifications — a strong re-engagement loop.
Price-drop tracking & history (M). Show "−10 000 MAD depuis 2 semaines" on detail. Urgency + trust.
Side-by-side compare (S–M). You have a compare.* lang namespace already — looks half-built. Finish it: compare 2–3 cars on specs/price/km.
Better search: fuzzy + Arabic/French brand aliasing (M). "Mercedes/مرسيدس", "Classe C/C-Class". Map common FR/AR/EN spellings so search doesn't return empty.
Map view by city (M). Moroccan buyers filter hard by location (Casa vs Marrakech). annonces.city is a name; a simple city-grouped map/list view fits.
Monthly-payment-first browsing (S, reuses financement). Let buyers filter by "mensualité < 3 000 MAD" instead of price — you already compute monthlies on cards. Huge for this market.

-5. Auto-Enchère (B2B auctions) — you already gate it; grow it
Live auction mode with real-time bidding (L). Currently it's listings-based; a true timed live auction (WebSockets/Pusher) for fleet liquidation events drives urgency and higher prices.
Proxy/max bids & outbid notifications (M). Standard auction UX that materially lifts final prices.
Fleet seller onboarding portal (M). Self-serve bulk upload (CSV/Excel — you already have GenericExport, build the import side) for leasing companies/banks dumping fleets.
Buyer deposit / pre-qualification (M). Only vetted, deposit-backed pros can bid — protects sellers, which attracts more fleet inventory.

10. Community & content (demandes, blog, radio)
Reviews & ratings for sellers/dealers (M). Post-deal rating builds the reputation layer the whole marketplace trust story needs.
Q&A on listings (S). Public buyer questions + seller answers (like Amazon) — reduces repetitive DMs, adds SEO content.
Model guides & owner reviews (content/SEO, M). "Dacia Duster au Maroc : avis, prix, fiabilité." Massive organic search opportunity in a market with little good FR/AR content. Feeds your blog.
Nado Radio → richer engagement (S). It's a nice differentiator; add podcast segments (buying tips, market updates) to increase dwell time.
